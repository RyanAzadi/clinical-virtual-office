Agent role, rules & data limitations

Patient Follow-Up and Access Improvement Agent: identify documented follow-up gaps, flag possible access barriers, prioritize managerial review, and suggest responsible teams. This application uses transparent rules and keyword matching; it does not call a generative AI model. Comments are treated as data, never as instructions.

Completed: yes, true, 1, completed, compliant. Not Completed: no, false, 0, incomplete, not completed, noncompliant. Other values are Unknown. Blank or missing data never means noncompliance. High priority requires incomplete follow-up plus a source High readmission-risk value or a comment with a configured possible-harm term. Medium means incomplete follow-up or an access signal. Low means completed with no access signal. Otherwise: Needs Clarification. Unknown status always requires clarification.

Barrier matching identifies possible signals, not confirmed causes. Age, persona and portal usage do not determine barriers or priority. Clinical concerns and any nonempty AI risk flag except explicit none/no/false require human review. Source risk labels are not recalculated. No diagnosis, treatment recommendation, outreach, or complaint resolution is performed.

Rates exclude unknown follow-up status. Group sizes and denominators are displayed; small groups may be misleading. Comparisons are descriptive and do not establish cause. Files are processed in this browser tab and are not uploaded to a server or retained after refresh. Names and contact fields are not displayed or exported. Use de-identified class data.

CSV headers supported: patient_id, service_line, visit_type, follow_up_compliance, patient_comments, readmission_risk, ai_risk_flags. Spaces and underscores are interchangeable. Required: follow_up_compliance. Other fields are optional. One row is treated as one record, not necessarily a unique patient.
